Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 9, 2026

Cursor Time Tracker ("we", "our", or "the extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our Chrome extension.

1. Information Collection

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII). We do not track your browsing history, and we do not have any external servers to receive your data.

2. Data Storage

The extension uses chrome.storage.sync to save your settings (such as your focus goal, custom icons, and counter values). This data is stored locally within your Chrome browser profile and is only used to maintain your preferences across browser sessions and synced devices (if you are logged into Chrome).

3. Data Sharing

Because we do not collect any data, we do not share, sell, or trade any information with third parties.

4. Permissions

The extension requires the following permissions to function:

  • Storage: To save your goals and counter counts.
  • Host Permission (<all_urls>): To display the floating cursor tracker on the websites you visit.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at theikbhal@gmail.com.
